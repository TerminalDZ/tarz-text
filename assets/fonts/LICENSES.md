## Size rule

`size_mm` is the height of the isolated alef (ا) for text containing
Arabic, and the capital height for other text. `min_size_mm` is the
recommended minimum for clean stitching.

## Pre-digitized `.tzf` files

`<id>.tzf` files are generated from the fonts above by the font-library
pipeline (`scripts/fonts/build.py`, converter `tarz_text::tzf`). They are
data derived from the font outlines and are distributed under the same
licence as their source font. They identify the source font only by its
Tarz id; do not market them under a Reserved Font Name.
